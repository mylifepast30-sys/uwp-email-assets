Printing: Use white paper (the colors print from the file — colored paper will distort the OSHA header colors). Turn ON "Background graphics" in the print dialog. Laminate each sheet, round the corners with scissors, and mount at about 60–66″ to sign center.

Where each sign goes is printed in small text at the bottom of each page — trim it off before mounting or fold it behind.

Heads up: Laminated paper is fine indoors. For the yard, docks, and the outdoor dust collector, these will fade — use them now, but budget for aluminum versions of the outdoor signs. Illuminated EXIT signs over exit doors are a fire-code item that paper can't replace. See OSHA-SIGN-TAKEOFF.md for the full list and quantities.
