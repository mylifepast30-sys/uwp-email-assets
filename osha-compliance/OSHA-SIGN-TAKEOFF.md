# OSHA Safety Sign & Compliance Takeoff
## Unique Wood Products — Warehouse / Shop

**Prepared:** August 2026
**Basis:** Shop photos (Uwp shop images folder): metal warehouse buildings, fenced Receiving/Shipping yard with double gates, brick front office, numbered dock doors (5, 6+), Fire Pump & Electrical Room, and new outdoor Nederman dust-collection system (baghouse, 150 HP Techtop fan, ATEX rotary valves, caged access ladder, elevated platform, outdoor electrical disconnects/panels).

**How to use this takeoff:** Quantities marked ✔ are counted from the photos. Quantities marked (V) are typical for a shop this size — verify the final count on a 30-minute walk-through (count doors, panels, extinguishers, and machines, then adjust).

**Sign standards (OSHA 1910.145 / ANSI Z535.2):**
| Header | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| DANGER | Red band, white letters | Hazard that will cause death/serious injury |
| WARNING | Orange band, black letters | Could cause death/serious injury |
| CAUTION | Yellow band, black letters | Could cause minor/moderate injury |
| NOTICE | Blue band, white letters | Policy / property protection |
| SAFETY FIRST / green signs | Green band | First aid, eyewash, safety equipment |

Print on white paper with the correct header colors, laminate, and mount 60–66" to sign center where practical. **Houston-area workforce: print bilingual English/Spanish** (the print pack in this folder is already bilingual).

# What You Need Besides Signs

## 1. Required postings (print these too — free)
- **OSHA "Job Safety and Health: It's the Law" poster** (OSHA 3165, English + Spanish) — osha.gov/publications/poster. Must be posted where employees see it (breakroom).
- **Texas Workers' Compensation coverage notice** (Notice 6 if covered, Notice 5 if non-subscriber) — English + Spanish.
- **Federal labor law posters** (FLSA minimum wage, USERRA, EEO, FMLA if 50+ employees).
- **OSHA Form 300A summary** posted every Feb 1 – Apr 30 (if more than 10 employees) — plus keep the OSHA 300 log year-round.
- **Emergency contact numbers** by phones/breakroom.

## 2. Written programs OSHA will ask for (binder on a shelf)
| Program | Standard | Applies because |
|---|---|---|
| Hazard Communication + SDS binder | 1910.1200 | Finishes, glues, solvents — label every container (GHS), SDS accessible to all shifts |
| Lockout/Tagout (energy control) | 1910.147 | Dust collector, CNC, all machines — written machine-specific procedures |
| Emergency Action & Fire Prevention Plan | 1910.38/39 | Written if >10 employees |
| PPE Hazard Assessment (signed/certified) | 1910.132 | Documents why you require glasses/hearing/gloves |
| Forklift operator training & certification | 1910.178 | Written cert per operator, refresh every 3 years + daily inspection checklist |
| Hearing Conservation (if ≥85 dBA TWA) | 1910.95 | Get a $200 noise survey done — planers/saws/dust fan usually exceed it |
| Respiratory Protection (if spraying) | 1910.134 | Fit tests + medical evaluations |
| Combustible Dust Housekeeping / DHA | NFPA 652/664 + General Duty | Schedule cleaning so dust never accumulates over 1/8"; no dry-sweeping with compressed air; document the Dust Hazard Analysis for the new collector system |

## 4. Recurring routines (put on the calendar)
- **Daily:** forklift pre-shift inspection checklist (keep the sheets).
- **Weekly:** eyewash flush; dust housekeeping walk (no >1/8" accumulation on beams/ledges/motors).
- **Monthly:** fire extinguisher visual check (initial tags); first aid kit restock; inspect cords/GFCIs.
- **Annually:** extinguisher professional service; LOTO procedure audit; forklift refresher as needed; hearing tests if in hearing program.
- **Training records for everything** — if it isn't documented, to OSHA it didn't happen.

## 5. Notes specific to your photos
- The new Nederman system with ATEX-rated rotary valves is a strong start on combustible-dust compliance — keep the documentation from Nederman (design basis, explosion isolation) in your DHA binder.
- The caged ladder + yellow platform railings look new and compliant; just add the "authorized personnel" and confined-space signage from Section A.
- The existing gate "Receiving & Shipping" sign is good; add the visitor check-in and forklift-traffic signs beside it.
- Exterior signs: laminated paper is a temporary fix outdoors — plan on aluminum for the yard, docks, and dust-collector signs.
