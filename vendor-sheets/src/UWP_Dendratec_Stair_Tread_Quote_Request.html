MILL SUPPLY SPECIFICATION

Species & Grade

  • Red Oak (Quercus rubra) · White Oak (Quercus alba)
  • Select Grade or better per CS 89-40. Face and nosing clear, free of sapwood.
  • Glued-up stock matched for color and grain. Max two streaks per tread, 6" max length.

Stave Construction

  • 3 to 5 staves maximum per tread, color-matched and grain-blended.
  • No visible contrast between adjacent staves. No heartwood-to-sapwood transitions on the face.
  • Glue joints tight, flush and continuous. Face grain consistent across all staves.

Face, Nosing & Defects

  • Face and nosing smooth — no mill marks, chipped grain, torn fiber or skip milling.
  • Nosing consistent and sharp. No feathering or rollover at the profile edge.
  • Knots: sound and tight, 3/4" max dia., 2 per piece. Checks: 1/4 length max, no through splits. Holes: spot-worm 1/16" max, 1 per lineal foot.

Moisture, Labeling & Packaging

  • Kiln-dried to 6–8% EMC prior to machining.
  • Each bundle labeled: species, grade, size, MC, mill name.
  • Banded on pallets with edge protection, wrapped loads only. End-coat all lengths 60"+.
  • 8071 / 8073 — full plastic wrap required (see above).
Technology exemption. Suppliers using certified moisture-management technology (engineered stabilization, sealed surface systems, or proprietary equivalent) may request a wrap exemption with prior written UWP approval. Documentation required before shipment.
